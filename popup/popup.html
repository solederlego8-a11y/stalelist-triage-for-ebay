StaleList Triage for eBay

Read-only triage for stale listings. It never ends, relists or edits your listings.

How to use

  1. Open Seller Hub → Active listings.
  2. Use the on-page panel (top right) to run triage.

No Seller Hub access? Open the bundled demo/demo.html page instead — the same panel works there with 12 sample listings.
