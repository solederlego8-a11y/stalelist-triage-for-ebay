# Privacy Policy — StaleList Triage for eBay

Last updated: 2026-11-01 (MVP draft — publish on a public URL before store submission)

## What we collect

**Nothing.** StaleList Triage does not collect, store remotely, sell, or
share any personal data or listing data.

- Listing information (titles, prices, views, watchers, etc.) is read from
  the page you have open, processed **entirely inside your browser**, and
  never transmitted anywhere.
- The extension stores the following **only on your device**
  (Chrome local storage), so the features work between pages and sessions:
  - your license key and its verification status (if you enter one),
  - your saved custom triage rule (Pro),
  - your cross-page selection (item IDs, verdicts, titles) until you clear it.
- We run no analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting, and no
  third-party scripts.

## The only data that ever leaves your device

- **License key verification (currently NOT CONNECTED).** When license
  verification is enabled, the *only* value sent is the license key string
  you entered, sent to our payment provider's verification endpoint to
  confirm your subscription. No listing data, browsing history, or personal
  information is included. In the current development build this verification
  is not connected and **no request is sent at all**.

## Limited Use disclosure

Our use of information received from the pages you view complies with the
Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:
data read from the Seller Hub page is used solely to provide the extension's
single, user-facing purpose (triage verdicts displayed to you on that page),
is never transferred to third parties, never used for advertising, and never
read by humans.

## Data retention & deletion

Everything is local. Removing the extension, clearing its storage, or using
the panel's "Clear saved selection" button deletes the stored data. The
license key can be cleared from the toolbar popup at any time.
